# Undergraduate CS, AI, and supporting math subjects

Retrieved **2026-11-08** from the [live MIT Course 6 catalog](https://catalog.mit.edu/subjects/6/) and [6-3_2026 track lists](https://eecsis.mit.edu/degree_requirements.pcgi?program=6-3).

This focused inventory contains **65 undergraduate subjects**. It selects named CS/AI/math areas and adds undergraduate subjects from the CS tracks, including cross-department choices. It is a menu, not a list to take in full. See [scope and degree requirements](README.md) and [the self-study proposal](self-study-plan.md).

Prerequisites are preserved from the catalog. A listed prerequisite is not satisfied merely by watching related videos. Catalog term fields are kept in [the JSON snapshot](catalog-snapshot.json), including future-year entries. Public lectures, labs, and enrollment are separate availability questions.

The wider JSON snapshot contains all 225 Course 6 undergraduate entries, including electrical engineering and temporary special subjects.

## Graduate extensions

6.5840 (older 6.824) distributed systems, 6.5660 systems security, and 6.5940 efficient deep learning are graduate-level options in the live track lists. They are excluded from the undergraduate inventory and can be considered after the relevant foundations.
