# Math foundations alongside Python

Prepared **2026-11-08**. Starting point: most school algebra, logarithms,
and calculus have been forgotten. Weekly budget: **5–7 hours of math**
within the overall 10–15 hours for the [self-study plan](self-study-plan.md).

## The sequence

Use worked problems to decide where to start. Forgetting algebra does not
necessarily mean arithmetic needs rebuilding too; check it and move on
quickly where it is already comfortable.

| Phase | Topics | Rough study weeks | Evidence before moving on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Arithmetic readiness | Negative numbers; fractions; decimals; percentages; order of operations. | 1–4, or skip after checking | Compute with fractions and signed numbers, estimate an answer, and explain each step. |
| 2 — Algebra | Variables; expressions; distribution; equations and inequalities; rearranging formulas; coordinates, slope, and simple systems. | 4–8 | Solve unfamiliar linear equations, substitute to check answers, and translate a short word problem into an equation. |
| 3 — Functions and growth | Function notation and graphs; powers and roots; quadratics and factoring; exponentials; logarithms as inverse operations. | 4–8 | Read a graph, evaluate and combine functions, explain exponential growth, and switch between logarithmic and exponential forms. |
| 4 — Calculus preparation | Radians; right-triangle ratios; unit circle; sine and cosine graphs; review algebra and functions together. | 3–6 | Use basic trigonometric relationships and solve mixed algebra/function problems without following an example line by line. |

These are adjustable estimates, not deadlines. Allow roughly **4–8 months**
for rebuilding the foundation with breaks and review; the first two weeks
will tell us whether to shorten or extend that range. Calculus comes after
this period and has its own study budget.

## Resources

Use one main exercise source rather than several parallel courses:

- [Khan Academy Arithmetic](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/arithmetic):
  review only the skills that need attention.
- [Khan Academy Algebra Basics](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/algebra-basics):
  main starting point after arithmetic readiness.
- [Khan Academy College Algebra](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/college-algebra):
  select functions, quadratics, exponents, and logarithms as needed; avoid
  repeating comfortable topics merely to finish another course.
- [Khan Academy Precalculus](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/precalculus):
  select trigonometry and readiness topics once algebra is secure.

For a written reference with exercises, use
[OpenStax Algebra and Trigonometry 2e](https://openstax.org/books/algebra-and-trigonometry-2e/pages/1-introduction-to-prerequisites).
Its [preface](https://openstax.org/books/algebra-and-trigonometry-2e/pages/preface)
confirms free web and PDF access. It is a reference to consult as needed,
not a second book to work through simultaneously.

MIT's [18.01SC syllabus](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-01sc-single-variable-calculus-fall-2010/pages/syllabus/)
specifies high-school algebra and trigonometry as prerequisites. It is the
next calculus course once the readiness checks above are comfortable.

## A useful practice session

Work for 45–60 minutes: recall something from the previous session, read
or watch one explanation, then solve problems on paper. Check the answers
after attempting them. Keep a short error log: the problem, the mistaken
step, and the correction. Retry missed problems on another day.

At each phase boundary, aim to solve at least 8 of 10 mixed practice
problems correctly on two different days, without hints, and explain why
the steps work. This is our planning heuristic, not an official course
requirement. Persistent confusion about the same idea matters more than
an isolated arithmetic slip.

The artifact for each phase is a small set of worked problems and corrected
mistakes. Keep earlier topics in the practice mix so recent familiarity
does not get mistaken for lasting understanding.

## First two weeks

Start with a few arithmetic exercises covering fractions, negative numbers,
and percentages. Review the weak areas; if these are comfortable, begin
expressions and one-step equations immediately. Spend the Python allocation
on the first lectures and problem set 0 at a manageable pace.

At the two-week checkpoint, use the worked exercises and actual time spent
to choose the next topics. Calculus and logarithms do not need to be restored
before learning variables, branching, and loops in Python.

After this foundation, the broader math route is calculus, discrete math,
linear algebra, and probability, sequenced with the chosen CS courses.
We can sample logic and simple proofs earlier, while keeping the formal
prerequisites of each full course visible.
